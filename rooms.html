Four Distinct Sanctuaries

Designed for Seclusion & Comfort

Every room at Ceylon Haven is positioned to maximize cross-ventilation, natural light, and serene garden or ocean vistas.

48 m² Tropical Garden View
Category 01

Garden Deluxe Room

Tucked within our lush botanical grove of palms, heliconias, and frangipani blossoms, the Garden Deluxe Room is a tranquil haven of earth tones and organic textures. Hand-carved teak furnishings are paired with crisp natural linen sheets and local woven tapestries.

Step onto your private shaded veranda to enjoy the morning birdsong or unwind in the private semi-outdoor rain shower garden enclosed by natural limestone walls.

  • Bed Type: Solid Teak King Bed (Pillow-top mattress)
  • Maximum Occupancy: 2 Adults (or 2 Adults + 1 Infant)
  • View: Verdant Tropical Botanical Gardens & Courtyard
  • Key Facilities: Open-air stone rain shower, private veranda with daybed, organic Ayurvedic herbal bath amenities, French press Ceylon coffee & single-origin tea bar, silent inverter air conditioning, high-speed fiber Wi-Fi.
Rate Indication $220 - $260 USD / night *Sample illustrative rate including organic breakfast & afternoon tea.

56 m² Panoramic Ocean View
Category 02

Ocean View Room

Perched on our upper terrace levels, the Ocean View Room commands uninterrupted vistas of the Indian Ocean and the golden curve of Bentota Beach. Expansive floor-to-ceiling louvered glass doors open to a generous private balcony equipped with a sunset daybed and rattan armchairs.

Drift to sleep to the rhythmic lullaby of ocean waves and wake to coastal amber dawns, enjoying fresh coconuts served directly to your balcony.

  • Bed Type: Four-Poster King Canopy Bed with sheer mosquito netting
  • Maximum Occupancy: 2 Adults
  • View: Panoramic Indian Ocean & Coastline
  • Key Facilities: Private ocean-facing sunset balcony, glass-encased rain shower with ocean vista, handcrafted terracotta minibar, Marshall Bluetooth speaker, organic linen bathrobes, in-room safe.
Rate Indication $280 - $340 USD / night *Sample illustrative rate including full Sri Lankan breakfast & sunset cocktails.

78 m² Direct Beachfront
Category 03

Premium Beach Suite

Located mere paces from the tideline, the Premium Beach Suite invites barefoot coastal living at its finest. Step off your private timber sun deck directly onto the soft golden sands of Bentota beach.

Featuring an expansive separate lounge area, a hand-carved stone soaking tub, and a private outdoor beach shower beneath swaying coconut palms, this suite embodies romantic coastal indulgence.

  • Bed Type: Ultra-Plush Super King Bed (300-thread count Egyptian cotton)
  • Maximum Occupancy: 2 Adults + 1 Child
  • View: Direct Beachfront & Ocean Waves
  • Key Facilities: Direct private beach pathway, timber sun deck with reserved double loungers, freestanding artisanal soaking tub, separate living salon, personalized packing & unpacking service.
Rate Indication $380 - $440 USD / night *Sample illustrative rate including daily gourmet breakfast & private beach cabana.

120 m² Private Plunge Pool
Category 04

Private Pool Villa

The pinnacle of exclusivity at Ceylon Haven: a standalone walled compound designed for total privacy and seclusion. Features a private 8-meter natural slate plunge pool, an outdoor dining pavilion, and manicured private courtyard gardens.

Includes dedicated 24-hour private villa host service to attend to in-villa dining, wellness scheduling, and exclusive customized excursions.

  • Bed Type: Master King Suite with hand-carved heritage headboard
  • Maximum Occupancy: 3 Adults (or 2 Adults + 2 Children)
  • View: Private Enclosed Tropical Courtyard & Pool
  • Key Facilities: Private freshwater plunge pool, dedicated private villa host (butler), alfresco dining sala, outdoor deep soaking tub, complimentary laundry, evening candlelight setup.
Rate Indication $480 - $580 USD / night *Sample illustrative rate including private host service, daily breakfast & airport transfer.
