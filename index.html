Welcome to Sanctuary

An Intimate Coastal Sanctuary in Bentota

Nestled where the tranquil Bentota River embraces the warm swells of the Indian Ocean, Ceylon Haven Boutique Retreat by Mavelle is an intimate luxury haven crafted for mindful travelers seeking peace, nature, and cultural depth.

Embracing sustainable tropical architecture, lush coconut groves, and indigenous teakwood craftsmanship, our boutique retreat offers an authentic expression of traditional Sri Lankan hospitality. Here, every sunrise begins with gentle sea breezes, every meal is nourished by organic island spices, and every twilight settles in secluded coastal tranquility.

Gastronomy

The Cinnamon Pavilion & Coastal Dining

Dining at Ceylon Haven celebrates the rich coastal harvests and aromatic spices of Sri Lanka. Our open-air restaurant, The Cinnamon Pavilion, overlooks the ocean garden, inviting guests to savor the delicate nuances of fresh cardamom, cinnamon bark, and fresh coconut milk.

From morning hopper stations with fiery seeni sambol to evening seafood barbecues directly on the sand illuminated by hurricane lanterns, our culinary team creates soulful, memorable dining experiences.

Our Commitment

Responsible Tourism & Sri Lankan Heritage

True luxury protects the land and community that inspires it.

🌊

Zero Single-Use Plastic

All water served across our retreat is purified on-site and bottled in reusable glass carafes. Bathroom amenities are 100% biodegradable and packaged in handcrafted terracotta pottery.

🐢

Sea Turtle Conservation

In collaboration with the nearby Kosgoda Turtle Conservation Project, we protect nesting sea turtles visiting Bentota beach and conduct ethical, educational guest releases.

🤝

Local Community First

Over 85% of our team members are residents of Bentota and neighboring fishing villages. We purchase all fish directly from local artisanal catamarans and produce from regional growers.

Destination

The Magic of Bentota

Situated along Sri Lanka's famed golden southern coastline, Bentota is renowned for its wide, unspoiled beach ribbon and the lush mangrove waterways of the Bentota Ganga River.

Just 90 minutes from Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) via the Southern Expressway, Ceylon Haven serves as the ideal launchpad for exploring the architectural masterworks of Geoffrey Bawa at Lunuganga Estate, river mangrove safaris, and day trips to the historic UNESCO World Heritage Galle Dutch Fort.
